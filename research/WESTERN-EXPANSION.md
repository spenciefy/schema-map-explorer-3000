# Western North America expansion

Scope: major destination resorts across the western United States and Canada. Includes 27 additional Ikon mountains, 10 Epic mountains/partners, and 5 independent destinations; Aspen is four separately explorable mountains. Smaller eastern and midwestern areas are intentionally outside this expansion.

Pass roster references checked September 18, 2026:
- https://www.ikonpass.com/en/compare-passes
- https://www.epicpass.com/regions.aspx
- https://www.epicpass.com/regions/partner-resorts.aspx

Pass categories indicate affiliation, not unrestricted admission. Specific products, dates and reservations vary.

Geometry is extracted from OpenStreetMap and Mapzen elevation tiles. ESA WorldCover supplies tree coverage. OSM timestamps are preserved per extract; this is a mapped snapshot, not live lift or trail status. Some larger domains use geographic windows. When matching OSM winter-sports boundaries are available, the enrichment script clips trails and lifts to those polygons. Roads and terrain retain the surrounding context.

Big Sky's official painted map was visually reviewed, particularly Lone Peak, the forest openings and Mountain Village approach. Its artistically unfolded perspective informs orientation only; trail coordinates remain geographic. Mammoth's front/back map pages, Vail's Front Side/Back Bowls/Blue Sky Basin pages, and Lake Louise's map resources were also consulted. New construction only appears if mapped in the source; no replacement alignments or seat counts are invented.
